## Research plans (after item 10; each is a short plan-first PR)

- **Overconfidence fix.** Today's models lose to the market. Build forecasters that start from the market price
  and shrink toward it with an a-priori weight (fixed before looking at results, e.g. 0.25), graded in the
  journal as separate forecasters so they never replace the pure model. Acceptance: they appear in `/models`
  as their own rows and the pure model's rows do not change.
- **Backtest evidence.** Replay the daily walk-forward models over history (check the `backtest_runs` schema
  first) and show the result as labelled context ("backtest, not counted") next to the live score. History
  never counts toward a gate; only frozen live forecasts do.

## Readability rules for any UI you touch

Numbers first (2-4 big stats), at most 15 words of intro, verdicts as 1-2 word chips, plain names, detail
behind a click, a missing figure is a word or a dash and never 0, and a test that caps the visible words.

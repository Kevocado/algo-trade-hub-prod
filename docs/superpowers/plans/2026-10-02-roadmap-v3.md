## Decisions already made (do not reopen)

- CPI display shows the frozen journal forecast, never a live recompute. The `/cpi` page therefore shows "Nothing locked in yet" most of the month: a CPI forecast exists only in the 24 hours before a report.
- Spread and total consumers come first; then calibration; then CPI; then the `predictions` writes stop (CPI and labor only).
- **Sports `predictions` writes are kept**, deliberately: they cover every rung of every ladder and are what the reviewer scorecard settles against. Only CPI and labor stop.
- One journal target per game and kind (the rung nearest a coin flip), so a ladder of 25 lines is one piece of evidence, not 25.

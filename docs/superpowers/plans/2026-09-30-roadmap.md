- Paste the plan's verification output in the PR body: the full backend suite at both `time.monotonic`
  clocks (5.0 and 1e7), ruff on the files you created, vitest/tsc/build for frontend changes.
- After opening a PR, stop and report in at most 4 lines. The reviewer comments within about 10 minutes.
